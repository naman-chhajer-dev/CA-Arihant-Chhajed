About

The Professional Behind the Precision.

Behind every calculation is a commitment to accuracy. Behind every recommendation is a responsibility to earn and maintain your trust.

I'm CA Arihant Chhajed, a Fellow Chartered Accountant (FCA) and a member of the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI). He holds a Bachelor of Commerce (B.Com) degree, is a qualified DISA (Diploma in Information Systems Audit) professional, and serves as an Executive Member of the Managing Committee of the Pali Branch of ICAI (CIRC). Since qualifying as a Chartered Accountant in 2016, he has built extensive experience in taxation, auditing, accounting, GST compliance, and financial advisory, with valuable professional exposure in Mumbai.

In 2020, I co-founded Chhajed & Panwar, a Chartered Accountancy firm based in Pali, Rajasthan, with the vision of delivering reliable, transparent, and client-focused financial services. Today, he provides comprehensive solutions in taxation, auditing, accounting, GST, and business advisory, helping individuals, professionals, startups, and businesses achieve financial clarity, regulatory compliance, and sustainable financial growth through a personalized and professional approach.

How I Can Help.

01

Income Tax Filing

ITR preparation and filing for salaried individuals, freelancers, NRIs and businesses, with tax-saving review before submission.

02

GST Registration & Returns

End-to-end GST registration, monthly/quarterly return filing, reconciliation and notice handling.

03

Statutory & Tax Audits

Audits for companies, LLPs and firms under the Companies Act and Income Tax Act, delivered without last-minute scrambling.

04

Business Registration

Company, LLP, partnership and proprietorship setup, including PAN, TAN, MSME and licence registrations.

Bookkeeping & Accounting

Financial & Tax Advisory

Structuring advice for investments, succession and business decisions with tax consequences in mind, not as an afterthought.
